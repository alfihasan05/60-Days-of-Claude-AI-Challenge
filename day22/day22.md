## 📌 Project Overview

For Day 21, I built a **Startup Validation System** designed to evaluate a startup idea before investing significant time, money, and resources.

The system analyzes:

- Problem validation
- Customer demand
- Founder-market fit
- TAM / SAM / SOM
- Competitors
- Market gaps
- Ideal Customer Profile
- Buyer personas
- Customer pain points
- Buying triggers
- Customer objections
- Customer journey
- Startup risks
- Pivot opportunities
- Go / No-Go recommendation
- 30-day validation plan
