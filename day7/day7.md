# Day 7 – Claude Model Selection & Reasoning Effort

## Personalized Claude Strategy

### Primary Model
**Claude Sonnet**

### Why?
- Best balance of speed, quality, and reasoning
- Ideal for coding, learning, writing, and daily productivity

### Model Guide
- **Haiku:** Quick summaries, emails, simple tasks
- **Sonnet:** Coding, learning, content creation, planning
- **Opus:** Deep research, strategy, complex reasoning

### Recommended Effort
- **Low:** Simple queries
- **Standard:** Daily work ✅
- **High:** Complex coding & analysis
- **Max:** Critical research & strategic planning

## Workflow

| Task | Model | Effort |
|------|-------|--------|
| Learning | Sonnet | Standard |
| Coding | Sonnet | Standard |
| Debugging | Sonnet | High |
| Content Writing | Sonnet | Standard |
| Deep Research | Opus | Max |
| Quick Tasks | Haiku | Low |

## Key Learnings
- Match the model to the task.
- Use Standard effort for most work.
- Reserve High/Max for complex reasoning.
- Sonnet is the best all-round choice.
