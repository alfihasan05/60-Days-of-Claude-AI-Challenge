# Day 2 – What Is Prompt Engineering 

## 60 day of claudeAI Challenge

## Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering is the skill of communicating with AI effectively. Clear, detailed prompts produce more accurate, relevant, and useful responses.

---

## Before (Weak Prompt)

**Prompt:**
```
Write about productivity.
```

**Output:**
A general explanation of productivity with basic tips.

---

## After (Engineered Prompt)

**Prompt:**
```
Write a 250-word LinkedIn post about productivity for remote professionals. Include 3 actionable tips, a real-world example, a motivational tone, and end with a question that encourages engagement.
```

**Output:**
A structured LinkedIn post tailored to remote workers with actionable advice, engaging storytelling, and a call to action.

---

## Key Takeaway

Small improvements in your prompt can dramatically improve AI responses. Giving context, audience, format, and tone helps AI generate higher-quality results.
