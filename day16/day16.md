## 4. Profitability & Returns

### ROE

**ROE: ~48.8%**

ROE measures how efficiently a company generates profit from shareholders' equity.

Framework:

| ROE | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| >15% | Good |
| 10–15% | Average |
| <10% | Weak |

BSE's reported ROE is therefore **strong** under this framework.

### ROCE

**ROCE: ~64.6%**

ROCE measures how efficiently the company generates returns from the capital employed in its business.

BSE's reported ROCE is also significantly above the 15% benchmark.
