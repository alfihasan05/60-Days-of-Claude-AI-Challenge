# Day 8 – Build Your First AI-Powered Dashboard

## Project
🌍 Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Objective

Build an interactive AI-powered dashboard that analyzes environmental health using Air Quality Index (AQI) and Water Quality data.

---

## Features

- Interactive Dashboard
- AQI Comparison Charts
- PM2.5 & PM10 Visualization
- Environmental Health Score
- Air Quality Report Card
- Water Quality Analysis
- Health Risk Indicators
- Responsive Dark UI
- Personalized Recommendations
