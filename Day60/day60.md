Day 60 — Capstone Graduation | Final Day

Today marks the end of the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge.

For the final 10-day capstone, I built SmartSave** — a savings goal planner designed to help users understand how much they need to save, track their progress, and know whether they are on track.

Over these 10 days, I went through the complete software development lifecycle:

- Product discovery and requirements
- System design and architecture
- Project setup
- Core feature development
- Progress and status logic
- UX refinement
- Testing and debugging
- Production preparation
- Deployment and final release

The biggest takeaway for me wasn't just building the application.

It was learning how to take an idea, break it into smaller problems, make decisions, test those decisions, fix what breaks, and eventually ship something real.

Today, SmartSave reached **Version v1.0.0**.

This capstone brought together many of the skills I developed throughout the full 60-day challenge — AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, product thinking, AI-assisted development, workflows, debugging, deployment, and building real applications.

I'm finishing the challenge with a working product, but more importantly, with a much better understanding of how to build.

Thank you to @Anthropic, @ABTalksOnAI, and @AnilBajpai for creating this challenge.
