 🦈 Day 25 — AI Shark Tank Simulator
 Build an AI Startup Investment Simulator

> Pitch your startup to AI investors and discover whether they would **Invest, Reject, Acquire, or ask you to Come Back Later.**

 🎯 Project Overview

For Day 25 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I built an interactive **AI Shark Tank Simulator**.

The application simulates a startup pitch session with four different AI investor personas. Each investor evaluates the startup from a different business perspective.

The goal is to turn a startup idea into a realistic investor-style evaluation.

 🎤 Pitch Round

The founder presents the startup pitch to the four AI judges.

Each judge asks **2 questions**, creating a total of:

**8 investor questions**

The founder can answer each question before receiving the final evaluation.

# 📊 Investment Scorecard

The startup receives a score out of 100 across five dimensions.

| Evaluation Area | Score |
|---|---:|
| Market Potential | /100 |
| Innovation | /100 |
| Business Model | /100 |
| Execution | /100 |
| Investment Worthiness | /100 |

The final score combines all five dimensions.

---

# 💰 Investment Decision

The simulator generates one of four outcomes:

### 🟢 INVEST

The startup demonstrates strong investment potential.

### 🔴 REJECT

The startup needs significant improvement before becoming investment-ready.

### 🟡 ACQUIRE

The startup may have strategic value that makes acquisition attractive.

### 🔵 COME BACK LATER

The idea has potential but needs stronger validation, traction, or execution.
