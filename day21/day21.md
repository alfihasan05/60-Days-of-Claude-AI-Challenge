## 🚨 Risk Analysis

### Highest Estimated Risk Areas

- 🔴 Social exposure
- 🔴 Communication exposure
- 🔴 Search-intent exposure
- 🔴 Payment exposure
- 🟠 Photo/content exposure
- 🟠 Commerce exposure
- 🟠 Gaming exposure

### Main Risk Drivers

1. Broad coverage across many digital categories.
2. Multiple services belonging to the same ecosystem.
3. Potentially valuable search, payment and commerce signals.
4. Potential account-linking and cross-service correlation surface.
5. Large amount of potentially sensitive digital activity across different platforms.

- Camera

### Priority 4 — Reduce Account Linking

Avoid unnecessary linking of unrelated services where practical.

Check activity history, personalization and automatic deletion settings.

## ⚠️ Important Limitations

This dashboard does **not** access or claim access to:

- Private databases
- Private messages
- Browsing histories
- Transaction records
- Location histories
- Account contents
- Platform-internal profiles
- Any private user information

The analysis is based **only on the supplied list of services**.

Behavioural, demographic, lifestyle, shopping, spending, entertainment, mobility, travel and technology-related conclusions are **Estimates**.

When information cannot reasonably be inferred:

> **Not enough information provided.**

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

1. A digital footprint can be analyzed without accessing private data.
2. Separating **Facts** from **Estimates** makes privacy analysis more responsible.
3. Service diversity can reveal the potential breadth of a digital footprint.
4. Ecosystem concentration helps identify where privacy controls may have greater impact.
5. Search, payments, photos and social interactions can represent potentially valuable data assets.
6. Privacy scores should be treated as heuristic indicators rather than absolute security measurements.
